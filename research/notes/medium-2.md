The LLM Landscape Part 1: The Starter’s Guide to Context Window Management
This starter's guide focuses on one of the most important LLM challenges: context window management for building real-world LLM applications.

The landscape of the Modern LLM Stack. Image by author.
Large Language Models (LLMs) are rapidly changing how we build software, work with knowledge, and automate complex tasks. At the same time, the LLM ecosystem is evolving so quickly that it is difficult to distinguish useful engineering tools from the noise.
Applications become more complex, and deciding what information to provide to an LLM, what to leave out, and how to structure and maintain context becomes critical for reliability, performance, and cost. In this blog, you will learn state-of-the-art practical approaches for managing context effectively, including context selection, compression, summarization, retrieval, memory, and techniques for avoiding context overload and the lost-in-the-middle phenomenon.
The goal is to provide a practical starting point for understanding and applying context window management when building real-world LLM applications. Each section introduces blogs and practical tools that you can try and use yourself.
